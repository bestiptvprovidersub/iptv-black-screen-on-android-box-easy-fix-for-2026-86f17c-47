IPTV black screen on Android box: easy fix for 2026

✍️ Matthew Hayes · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 4 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

Your IPTV app shows a black screen. This happens because of a wrong setting or a slow network.

Follow these steps to fix it in minutes. Most fixes take less than two minutes.

Common Causes and Fixes

Cause 1 → App or Device Needs a Restart

  1. Press the power button on your Android box remote for 10 seconds until it turns off.
  2. Unplug the power cord from the box. Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Plug it back in and turn on the box.
  4. Open your IPTV app again.

If the black screen is gone, the fix is done. If not, move to the next cause.

Cause 2 → Wrong Video Output Settings

  1. Go to Settings → Display & Sound → Resolution on your Android box.
  2. Set resolution to 1920×1080 (1080p) at 60 Hz.
  3. If you have a 4K TV, try 3840×2160 (4K) at 60 Hz, but only if your box supports it.
  4. Restart the IPTV app after changing the resolution.

Most Android boxes work best at 1080p. 4K can cause black screens on older boxes.

Cause 3 → Outdated IPTV App or Player

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android box (or use the Downloader app).
  2. Search for your IPTV player. For example: TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, Flex IPTV.
  3. If an update is available, tap Update.
  4. Reopen the app after the update.

Old versions may not support the latest video codecs like HEVC 4K 60 fps.

Cause 4 → Slow or Blocked Network (Use Cloudflare DNS)

Some internet providers slow down IPTV streams. A DNS change helps.

  1. Go to Settings → Network & Internet → Wi‑Fi or Ethernet.
  2. Tap on your network name, then choose Modify Network.
  3. Change IP settings from DHCP to Static.
  4. Set DNS 1 to 1.1.1.1 and DNS 2 to 1.0.0.1 (Cloudflare DNS).
  5. Save and reconnect to the network.

Cloudflare DNS makes your connection faster and avoids ISP throttling.

Cause 5 → Buffer / Cache Size Too Low

IPTV players need enough cache to load video smoothly. Set it to 5,000 ms.

  1. Open your IPTV app’s settings. For TiviMate, go to Settings → Playback → Buffer Size.
  2. Change the buffer to 5000 ms (sometimes shown as 5 seconds).
  3. Do the same in other players: look for Buffer, Cache, or Streaming Buffer.
  4. Restart the app.

A larger buffer prevents the black screen when your internet speed drops.

Cause 6 → Corrupted Cache in the App

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → See All Apps.
  2. Find your IPTV player (e.g., IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate).
  3. Tap Clear Cache and then Clear Data (this will delete your login info, so have it ready).
  4. Open the app and sign in again.

Clearing data fixes bugs that cause a black screen.

Cause 7 → Hardware Acceleration Issue

  1. Open your IPTV app’s settings.
  2. Look for an option named Hardware Acceleration or Decoder.
  3. Turn it OFF (or switch from “Auto” to “Software Decoder”).
  4. Restart the stream.

Some Android boxes have weak hardware decoders. Using software decoding solves black screens on those devices.
